# Quest preparation and recovery audit

Scope: all 63 modules in [QUEST_DEFS](../../src/bot/api/ai/quests/defs/index.ts), their decision paths, and the supply/recovery helpers called by those paths. This is a source audit, not a claim that every restart state has passed a live run. The matrix describes existing module behavior and remaining gaps; the shared preparation policy can cover a missing input without changing that module's local wait.

[Quest records](../../src/bot/api/ai/quests/data/quests.ts) describe eligibility, not a complete loadout. `ownsInventory` skips generic provisioning. Most modules deliberately source consumables at their current stage, because an unconditional preparation list would fetch ingredients already handed in. Unknown journals need a reread; an unread bank is not an empty bank. A failed custom step can mean navigation, dialogue, a random event, or depleted supplies, so it should not automatically become a permanent quest failure.

## Registry coverage

Requirements below distinguish recorded skill gates from extra sourcing gates. Quest prerequisites and quest-point gates remain in the records. Food counts are local targets, not proof that the same number of every food is equally safe. A blank combat requirement means no extra policy is declared in the module, not that travel is harmless.

## Shared fixes the audit calls for

1. Prepare from a fresh bank snapshot, worn gear, current journal stage, and actual module demands. Keep source-only skill requirements conditional on missing finished/intermediate supplies. Do not train Fishing 50 for Caleb if his fish are banked, or Fletching 65 for Ikov if the usable bow already exists.
2. An empty food bank needs a source plan, not `wait` or silent continuation. Underground Pass accepts only lobsters locally and Regicide only sharks; either supply those foods or change their entire count/keep/eat policy together. Substituting a food name in generic preparation leaves their local kit checks unsatisfied.
3. Replace bank-only waits for ordinary tools, food, potions, and craftable materials with acquisition/training work. Retain explicit partner, unsupported-branch, wilderness-disabled, and delivery waits. Item acquisition must preserve quest progress and resume the same quest after the preparation finishes.
4. Keep quest-owned inventory decisions in charge inside sealed areas and after item consumption. General preparation should stock a bank before departure, or wait for a verified safe exit; it must not send an in-progress character directly to an unreachable booth.
5. Protect exact IDs for keys, certificates, partial cages, herb/rum/vessel variants, and unique quest items. Name equality alone is unsafe. Preserve hand-in foods separately from survival food, and never drop an item merely because it is absent from the current generic tool list.
6. Separate expected waits/custom retries from unresolved requirements. Resource-grind XP, material count, journal flags, local movement, and recovery activity are evidence of progress even when the quest stage does not change.

## Combat policy evidence

There is no uniform minimum combat profile. [Elemental Workshop](../../src/bot/api/ai/quests/defs/elementalworkshop/supplies.ts) has recorded failed and successful profiles: Attack/Strength 40, Defence 25, HP 40 failed; 50/50/40/50 passed. [Family Crest](../../src/bot/api/ai/quests/defs/familycrest/supplies.ts) only cites a max-combat pass. Holy Grail, Tree Gnome Village, Observatory, Mort'ton and Legends cite 70s profiles. Those observations should remain evidence, not silently become server requirements.

Protection thresholds are Prayer 43 melee, 40 missiles, and 37 magic. Declared protection can inform a chosen preparation policy, but Troll Stronghold explicitly says its potions are optional and the quest is winnable on food alone. Legends' recorded Prayer 42 is sufficient for the bowl gate but not Protect from Melee at 43. Food amount, healing per slot, defence, prayer duration, and retreat route need to agree before a lower combat profile can be called supported.

High-priority live coverage: empty bank food, missing tool with a free source, skill training interrupted by a random event, mid-quest restart after consumed ingredients, death inside a sealed pocket, and banked unique-item recovery. This audit does not establish live success for those cases.

The shared preparation policy is deliberately narrower than this matrix: it prepares recorded skills, declared protection (except Troll Stronghold's optional strategy), supported food, and a small set of ordinary bank-only supplies. Waterfall and Tourist Trap keep their existing local food acquisition. It adds source skills only before starting and only when stock lacks the relevant product/intermediate. It does not invent combat floors from advisory profiles or source unique quest items before their NPC stages. Remaining module-specific recovery gaps above still need separate fixes and live coverage.

Hero's eel-source levels were also checked against the local content checkout: lavafish.rs2 requires Fishing 53, and brew_potion.struct defines blamish oil at Herblore 25. A banked raw eel bypasses fishing and oil preparation; an oily rod bypasses Herblore.
