# AccountLeveler methods and equipment

On a bank check the leveler prioritizes an owned clue or trail casket, then resumes queued work. Hard clue farming keeps its prerequisite and gear preparation for hard trails. Routine bank checks use the Bank Sorter ordering and keep quest items.

The default target is 70 in every enabled skill. Each account randomly draws its next task from its unfinished skills and eligible Lost City quest. If a task is blocked, the planner draws another. Queued supplies and prerequisites continue with their original objective. Supply gathering can exceed the target to support another skill. Slayer is disabled in this game revision.

The planner checks loaded bank contents, inventory, equipment, levels, quest completion, supplier failures and available gold before choosing work. It buys tools separately from choosing resources: a better pickaxe doesn't force a switch to a higher ore. Completed resource dependencies return to their queued objective.

## Lost City and dragon weapons

Lost City joins the rotation once Crafting reaches 31 and Woodcutting reaches 36. The planner prepares food and Strike runes, leaves melee equipment behind for Entrana, and reserves a way out of Zanaris: a Diamond, a cuttable Uncut diamond, or Varrock teleport supplies with Magic 25. Unavailable supplies defer the quest while other training continues.

After Dragon Slayer, the next bank check looks for rune weapon upgrades at 40 Attack and rune armour upgrades at 40 Defence. This check also runs in hard clue farming mode and keeps the shopping reserve.

At Attack 60 with Lost City complete and a Dramen staff, the leveler prioritizes Jakut's Dragon longsword at the next bank check. It uses a banked diamond or cuts an uncut one at Crafting 43. If it lacks the toll, it reserves the sword money and checks Seers Market for a diamond. It can also train Crafting and loot an uncut diamond. A dagger is an additional purchase when funds cover it. Queued melee training picks up the new longsword.

Combat sessions use supported weapon specials when enough energy is available, including dragon weapons and the magic shortbow. AutoFighter banks a full pack with loot to deposit and restocks earlier when food, ammunition or spell supplies run low. Combat sessions bank secondary drops such as limpwurt roots, herbs and runes for later skills; this also applies to dragon Prayer sessions.

When the same two other players stay within eight tiles of the account at its training spot for ten seconds, the leveler saves a 30-minute cooldown for that spot and replans. A supplied fighter can move directly to another eligible camp for the same NPC. The cooldown applies across training methods and survives a script restart. Banking and active clue steps pause crowd detection. Wilderness dragon hunting and fishing ignore crowding, including saved spot cooldowns.

Routine supply and bank trips avoid the Wilderness. Explicitly enabled Wilderness training remains available, and accounts already inside can still leave for a bank.

## Dragon Slayer and dragon bones

While melee or Prayer remains below target, accounts with Attack, Strength, Defence and Hitpoints 40 can prepare for Dragon Slayer. The planner completes supported solo quests until it has 32 quest points, skips completed quests, and respects quest cooldowns. It prepares Fishing and Cooking 40 when Lobsters are not stocked. Missing quest supplies can also require Smithing 34, Mining 30 and Crafting 8. Banked Nails and an Unfired bowl satisfy those material prerequisites directly.

Starting Dragon Slayer reserves 15,000 coins separately from equipment purchases. Resumed quests read the journal and account for consumed ship supplies. With Wilderness disabled, bring the required Planks; the delegated quest is also forbidden from gathering replacements in the Wilderness. Completing the quest enables owned Rune platebodies through the existing equipment selector.

About one in five Seers suppliers prioritizes dragon hunting, with a stable assignment based on character name. With Wilderness enabled, those suppliers bank hides and bones from green dragons. At 70 Attack, Strength, Defence and Hitpoints, roughly one in four dragon suppliers prefers adult red dragons on Red Dragon Isle. Either Wilderness camp can cover for the other during a cooldown if the account qualifies. Other suppliers choose eligible non-dragon work first and hunt dragons only if no other supply job is available. They sell full thousands of each drop independently. Prayer training rotates between eligible green, red and blue dragon sites. It disables clue detours and potion use, explicitly chooses Lobsters so another saved loadout cannot replace the food, and stops at the Prayer target. Blue dragons handle their own dusty-key acquisition. Both Wilderness camps use the same escape-to-bank behavior. Questing is part of unfinished combat progression; accounts whose combat targets are already complete keep the existing skilling and completion behavior.

## Magic and rune supplies

The leveler checks the bank for High Alchemy at Magic 55 and Superheat Item at Magic 43 before planning combat spells or a chaos rune run. On World 1 it can buy alchemy items from Seers when the quote covers the cost of the cast.

Accounts already at Thieving 70, Hitpoints 30 and melee stats 20 can pickpocket Ardougne Paladins for missing chaos runes. The trip prepares food, collects the requested rune batch and returns to the queued Magic activity, even when shops have marked chaos runes unavailable. Bank preparation reselects the spell and rune requirements for queued Magic activities, so an earlier Strike plan can upgrade to Bolt after gathering chaos runes or reaching the required Magic level. It does not train Thieving to unlock Paladins. Accounts unable to pickpocket Paladins still use owned chaos runes, with shop supplies and Strike spells as fallbacks.

### Seers market

Enable "Supply Seers Market only" on World 1 to start supplying immediately at any level. The bot banks its inventory, selects one eligible item with at least 1,000 banked, and offers every full thousand above its reserve in one trade. With 2,350 coal it offers 2,000 and leaves 350 banked. Each herb, hide, potion dose and piece of equipment counts separately. Each rune and finished arrow type has a 1,000-unit reserve and requires more than 2,000 banked to start a sale. With 2,001 steel arrows the bot sells 1,000. With 3,000 it sells 2,000. Between sales it rotates products and favors higher-tier work the account can do with its current skills and supplies. Within a tier it finishes the nearest lot. Feather runs stop at combat 15 and cowhide runs stop at combat 35. Market collection skips tin and copper ore. The Wilderness setting still controls access to Wilderness methods.

On World 1, the leveler asks the player `seers market` for missing activity supplies when its NPC shops cannot supply them. It rounds resource shortages up to the next multiple of 1,000 for each Seers order. This includes rune essence, runes, food, ores, bars, ammunition and other training supplies. Equipment, tools, talismans and the single Zanaris entry diamond use the requested quantity. A trade can spend all coins available after reserving travel money and the funds needed for the queued activity. The leveler banks purchases before packing its loadout. An unavailable seller, a stock refusal or a quote the account cannot afford puts the item on a one-hour cooldown for the account. An interrupted or timed-out trade is eligible to retry after one minute. Both deadlines survive restarts. Other items and training methods remain eligible.

The buyer waits for the maker's trade invitation. When the maker is fetching stock, the buyer waits for the line naming its character and item. When the maker names another customer it is serving, the buyer holds its place. A named partial fill uses the quoted unit price. For bulk supplies, the buyer requests a fresh quote for the largest available whole lot if the maker offers an uneven quantity. It skips offers below 1,000, including partial bank fetches. The buyer gives the maker up to four minutes to open a trade.

Ranged training can request a strung magic shortbow from level 50. Ranged combat and the guild competition select dragonhide by slot and colour at Ranged 40, 50, 60 and 70. Bodies require 40 Defence, and the green body also requires Dragon Slayer. Green pieces use the supported NPC shops when accessible.

The startup supply check and woodcutting sessions look for an owned Rune axe first. If one is missing, the leveler travels to Seers Market on World 1 before considering Bob. While a Rune axe purchase is on cooldown, the leveler selects an available axe, which can include buying a lower tier from Bob.

The leveler travels to Seers with the purchase money banked. Once the maker invites it to trade, it withdraws the quoted total at Seers bank. The activity reserve stays in the bank.

The seller's current presence and stock are not assumed. Market behavior is covered by simulated protocol tests; no live player trades are performed by the test suite.

## Budget and failure policy

Startup shopping defers Nurmof's underground shop below Hitpoints 25 or Defence 20; the tool is purchased when mining starts after combat training. Startup shopping shares a budget across the equipment and consumable list, retaining at least 200 coins or 10% of bank gold, whichever is larger. Individual provisioning trips reserve 200 coins for travel and verify inventory capacity and delivered items. Purchase budgets are conservative ceilings, not quoted NPC prices.

Rune and arrow provisioning tries both shops for any remaining shortfall, banking partial purchases between suppliers. A filled order skips the second shop.

Startup purchase targets are ceilings: buy the available shelf stock, bank it and continue, without repeated visits to chase the target. Training accepts smaller arrow/feather/thread batches and complete sets of spell runes, with child restock quantities matched to the actual loadout. The next refill rechecks normal targets so a small purchase does not permanently shrink future batches. When a shop sells out of an item, AutoLeveler waits a random 20–30 minutes before trying that item at that shop again. The timer survives restarts. It can use banked supplies, buy other items from the same shop, or try another supplier in the meantime. Unreachable vendors still allow owned gear or other available tiers.

Failures never finish the account leveler. Preparation retries from the bank twice, then discards the queued plan and replans; repeated preparation errors increase the retry delay from 3 seconds to a maximum of 60 seconds. Repeatedly failing combat or quest plans receive a 15-minute cooldown. Routine gathering resets twice, then replans after 30 seconds without cooling down food production. A combat camp that remains unreachable after navigation recovery is immediately cooled down for 15 minutes, allowing another camp to be selected.

When no method is currently possible, the leveler rechecks bank supplies and supplier availability at least every 30 seconds, or when an earlier cooldown expires. The blocking reason stays visible. Old gathering cooldowns are discarded, and the latest failure reason is saved separately from the short diagnostic history. On World 1, reaching every target starts Seers supply runs when "Supply Seers after target" is enabled. The leveler sells surplus ores, runes and Herblore secondaries, then rotates between gathering methods it can already run. It keeps 10,000 air runes, 1,000 each of fire, water, earth, law and nature runes, and 100 of each eligible ore or secondary. Each item sells in multiples of 1,000 above its reserve. Rune sales also require more than 2,000 of the same type banked. Any remainder stays banked. Disabling the setting finishes training at the target. Hard clue farming keeps its own completion rules.
